IBIS Teacher Core is the evolution of a fully integrated and intuitive online Student Management System or Student Information System for Online and Remote language teachers
IBIS, or Intelligently Building Online Study holds as its central philosophy the idea that education is all about learning but it is not just the student who must learn, the teacher must be open to learning 
and change too. It is often a mistaken belief that by being a teacher, one is static. 
Today one cannot be static. 
Teacher Core is built around a Google Calendar sync engine which polls lesson calendars, notes and corroborates changes, and ensures any changes are applied correctly. A student reschedules a lesson? TC 
will compare records and occurences and update that, preserving all other data while changing the date and the time. 
Its second strength is its persistent student and lesson memory. TC will monitor student changes over time and make reasoned suggestions. When paired with IBIS Lesson Recorder, it will upload and process
Evidence Files the Recorder produces and store the findings in a student's profile. Over time, we can monitor and react to the trend we see emerging. 
TC is built not only to make your every day tasks easier but also help you plan and grow with the future.
