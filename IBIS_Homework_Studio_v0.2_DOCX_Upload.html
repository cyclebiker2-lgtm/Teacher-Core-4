Worksheet design rules

  • CEFR level and vocabulary complexity should remain appropriate to the learner.
  • Exercises should draw on the source text when Option A is selected.
  • Grammar practice should target the points you specify, not unrelated grammar.
  • Keep student-facing tasks separate from teacher answers.
  • Include clear instructions, examples where useful, and varied answer patterns.
  • In Option A, include a concise summary of the source text. In Option B, include a new text of approximately 300 words.
