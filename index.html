Calendar timezone
Fixed manual offset. IBIS treats imported lesson times as GMT and currently adds two hours. When the clocks change, switch this setting to GMT+1 and IBIS will recalculate the stored calendar display.

Incremental Sync v3 bootstraps a Google sync cursor, then checks only calendar changes. Changes are queued for preview; applying remains explicit. Cancellations and uncertain matches are flagged, not silently merged. Lesson IDs, preparation, reports and historical evidence must be preserved.
